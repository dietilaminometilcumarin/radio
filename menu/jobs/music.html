Как это работает?

We've combined high-quality broadcasting and blockchain technology to give content creators complete financial independence. No more complex contracts and long payment waits—monetize your work directly through your listeners.

Step 1: Bot @bot_radio

Install the Bitcord messenger at link.digci.com. Search for our specialized "Artist to Radio" bot using the login @bot_radio.

Step 2: Profile and Tracks

Use the bot to upload your personal profile information (author picture and bio), your audio recordings, and their descriptions. Payment for placement and moderation is made in the messenger's internal cryptocurrency.

Step 3: Radio Rotation

Once approved, your copyrighted material goes directly to Radio Hi-Fi and becomes available for rotation. Royalties are automatically credited to your Messenger wallet as long as people listen to your track.

4. Step: Dynamic Chart

The system is completely transparent: if a track loses listener interest, it moves down, making way for newer compositions, which also begin to monetize.

Withdraw to real money

All earned royalties accumulate in your balance. You can instantly withdraw them to your personal non-custodial wallet (e.g., Metamask) and then easily exchange them for real money.

Authorship Registration

We are developing a special smart contract. With it, each author will be able to officially register their authorship on the blockchain. Royalties will be automatically distributed among all participants in the track's creation (musicians, producers, lyricists).

Build your community on Bitcord

We highly recommend musicians download the messenger, register, and create their public communities right away! Bring your fans into the Bitcord ecosystem.

What does this mean? Fans will be able to communicate with you directly, without intermediaries, and vote for your tracks right within the messenger. Every vote directly influences the promotion of your music in the rotation on Hi-Fi Radio!

The most active listeners and fans will be rewarded with commemorative artist NFTs, which they can resell for the same internal cryptocurrency, creating a full-fledged economy around your work.
